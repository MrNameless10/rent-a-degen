Rent a degen in your browser

The real app runs in Lua 5.4 (WebAssembly) on a simulated Solana OS, with mesh between three badges, !LAMPs and the system payment dialog. Click a badge and use ↑↓←→, Enter SELECT and Esc CANCEL. Tab switches badge. Text is typed on the on-screen keyboard, as on the badge: arrows to choose, SELECT types, CANCEL deletes and ✓ confirms.
